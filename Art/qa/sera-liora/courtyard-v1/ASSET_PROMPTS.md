A small planting border and bench can occupy the far upper right x310..500,y190..255, and tasteful flower planters at extreme edges only. Lower edge y430..480 can have rich leafy foreground planting, with a clearly cut silhouette against paving for depth masking. On right side imply a continuation toward town beyond a stone arch at x470,y310 but do not show another playable area. No second prominent door. Runtime geometry follows the accepted pixels, not the prompt's proposed coordinates: foot arrival `(166,254)`, a short door apron, and clear paved floor below. This is an implementation layout decision, not new lore.
