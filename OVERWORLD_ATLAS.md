Wetwoods extends west of Calwick as inaccessible wooded scenery. Thornmere now fills a sheltered basin in the sealed southern reserve, connected to the old northern shallows. Its reed shore and wooded margins use existing tiles. No starting-area access or camera policy changes. The southwestern countryside is sealed from ordinary early-game travel at Thornmere Fen. Its remaining shores stay reserved and debug-accessible; submerged cells and the four open-water chunks are no longer valid placements. Placement-enabled totals include sealed reserve shores. Northern Road/Blocked Path keep their west camera edge, and Eastern Reaches/Thornmere Fen keep their south camera edge.

Valmere’s wilderness shore uses irregular reed banks, wooded points and gravel coves. This scenery-only refinement preserves every playable grid, Mornay’s peninsula and harbour, river mouths and all camera/access rules.

Mornay’s lakeward approach is a modest one-to-two-tile road with grassy verges. Its compact town marker shows roofs, a northwestern fort and a western harbour; only the two southern gates enter town. The town interior and camera policies are unchanged.
